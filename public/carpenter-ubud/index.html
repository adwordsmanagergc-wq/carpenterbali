Carpenter in Ubud

Ubud is Bali's cultural and wellness heart, surrounded by jungle, rivers and rice terraces.

WhatsApp usGet a free quote

We build custom kitchens, wardrobes, teak doors, furniture and complete villa interiors in Ubud, Penestanan, Sayan, Mas and Tegallalang.

Local conditions: Ubud

Ubud is cooler but much wetter and more humid than the coast, with jungle humidity that encourages mould and termites. Ventilation, termite treatment and fully sealed timber are essential here.

Popular styles in Ubud

Natural and handcrafted: solid teak, bamboo, rattan, recycled timber, carved details and earthy finishes that blend into the jungle.

Homes & projects in Ubud

Ubud homes range from jungle villas and eco-retreats to long-term family houses, yoga retreats and boutique hotels, many with natural, open designs.

How it works

  1. Consultation

    Share photos, plans or inspiration on WhatsApp. We discuss style, budget and timing.

  2. Site visit & measure

    We measure on site, check walls, floors and moisture, and advise on materials.

  3. Design & quote

    You receive drawings or a 3D render plus an itemised written quote.

  4. Workshop build

    Your pieces are built and finished in our workshop. We send progress photos.

  5. Installation

    Our own team installs, adjusts and cleans up. We walk you through care and maintenance.

  6. Aftercare

    Workmanship warranty and a check-up visit to adjust hinges and doors after the first wet season.

Frequently asked questions

How do you deal with Ubud's high humidity?

We use well-seasoned timber and marine-grade boards, seal all faces, add ventilation to wardrobes and cabinets, and treat everything against termites.

Do you cover villages around Ubud?

Yes, Penestanan, Sayan, Kedewatan, Mas, Peliatan, Lodtunduh, Tegallalang and Keliki.

Do you charge for a site visit and quote?

No. Site visits and quotes are free across our service areas in south Bali and Ubud. For remote locations we may agree a small travel fee in advance.

What deposit do you require?

Typically 50% to start production, 40% when pieces are ready for installation and 10% on completion. Exact terms are written in your quote.

Can you work from my architect's or designer's drawings?

Yes. We regularly build from architects' and interior designers' drawings and can prepare shop drawings for approval before production.
