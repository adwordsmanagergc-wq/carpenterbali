Timber staircases in Legian

Planning a staircase in Legian? Carpenter Bali builds floating stairs, timber stair cladding and railings for villas and homes in Legian.

WhatsApp us Get a free quote

A staircase is often the first thing you see in a villa. We build floating stairs with solid teak or merbau treads, clad concrete stairs in timber, and make complete timber staircases for lofts and mezzanines.

We combine timber with steel stringers, glass balustrades, rope or slatted railings to suit tropical-modern, industrial or classic Balinese interiors — always designed for safety and stability.

Local conditions: Legian

Close to the beach, Legian properties face salt air and humidity. We replace failing chipboard joinery with marine-grade materials built to last.

Popular styles in Legian

Clean modern designs with timber accents are most popular in Legian, as owners refresh older properties for today's guests.

Homes & projects in Legian

Legian has many older villas and hotels ready for renovation, as well as compact apartments where clever built-in storage makes a big difference.

What we build

Floating stairs

Thick solid treads cantilevered from the wall or on a hidden steel spine.

Stair cladding

Timber treads and risers fitted over concrete stairs.

Loft & mezzanine stairs

Space-saving and alternating-tread designs.

Railings & balustrades

Timber, steel, glass, rope or slatted screens.

Spiral stairs

Compact spiral and helical stairs for tight spaces.

Anti-slip finishes

Grooved nosings and anti-slip oils for safety.

Materials & finishes

Solid teak, merbau or bengkirai treads (40–80 mm); steel stringers or spines; tempered glass, steel or timber balustrades; hard-wearing polyurethane or oil finishes.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a floating staircase cost in Legian?

Floating stairs in Legian typically start from around Rp 2.5–5 million per step, including treads and fixings. Cladding existing stairs starts from about Rp 1.2 million per step.

How long does stair installation take in Legian?

Usually 2–4 days on site after 4–7 weeks of fabrication.

Can you clad my existing concrete stairs in wood?

Yes. We measure each step individually and make solid timber treads and risers that are fixed and sealed onto the concrete.

Are floating stairs safe?

Yes, when engineered and fixed correctly. We use thick solid treads and fix them into structural walls or a steel spine, and recommend a balustrade wherever there's a drop.

Can you renovate an older villa in Legian?

Yes. We replace kitchens, wardrobes, vanities and doors to bring older Legian villas up to date.

Do you build space-saving storage for apartments in Legian?

Yes. We design built-in wardrobes, beds with storage and compact kitchens for apartments.
