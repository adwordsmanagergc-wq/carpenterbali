Ceilings & wall panelling in Seminyak

Want warmth and texture in your space in Seminyak?

WhatsApp usGet a free quote

Carpenter Bali installs timber ceilings, slatted feature walls and acoustic panels in villas, restaurants and offices in Seminyak.

Timber ceilings and wall panels add warmth, texture and acoustics to any space. We install tongue-and-groove and slatted ceilings, feature walls behind beds and TVs, acoustic slat panels for restaurants and offices, and exterior cladding.

Choose from solid teak, merbau, bamboo, pine or veneer panels, with integrated lighting, hidden access hatches and ventilation for the tropics.

Materials & finishes

Teak, merbau, pine, bamboo or veneered plywood; treated timber battens; acoustic felt backing; oil, lacquer or stain finishes.

Local conditions: Seminyak

Seminyak's beachfront location means salt-laden air and high humidity. Hardware corrosion and swelling of cheap boards are common problems we fix, and prevent with the right materials.

Popular styles in Seminyak

Seminyak favours refined luxury: dark teak with brass, terrazzo and marble, boutique-hotel style walk-in closets, statement doors and polished commercial interiors.

Homes & projects in Seminyak

Seminyak properties range from established luxury villas due for renovation to compact private-pool villas run as holiday rentals, plus a dense mix of restaurants, bars and boutiques.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a timber ceiling cost in Seminyak?

Timber slat ceilings in Seminyak start from around Rp 600,000-1.2 million per m², and bamboo ceilings from about Rp 350,000 per m².

How long does panelling installation take in Seminyak?

Most rooms are completed in 2-5 days on site after fabrication.

Will a wooden ceiling attract termites?

We treat all timber and battens with anti-termite preservative and leave inspection access. Combined with a professional termite barrier for the building, timber ceilings are very durable.

Can wall panels improve acoustics?

Yes. Slatted panels on acoustic felt noticeably reduce echo in restaurants, offices and open-plan villas.

Do you renovate older villas in Seminyak?

Yes. We often replace tired kitchens, wardrobes and doors in older Seminyak villas to increase rental appeal.

Do you do restaurant and boutique fit-outs in Seminyak?

Yes. We build bar counters, banquettes, shelving and display joinery for hospitality and retail businesses in Seminyak and Petitenget.
