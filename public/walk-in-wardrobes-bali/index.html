Walk-in wardrobes & dressing rooms in Bali

Luxury walk-in closets and dressing rooms with islands, lighting and open or glass-fronted storage.

A walk-in wardrobe turns getting dressed into a daily pleasure. We design and build bespoke walk-in closets and dressing rooms for villas and family homes across Bali — from a compact walk-through between bedroom and ensuite to a boutique-style dressing room with a central island, display shelving and statement lighting.

In the tropics, a walk-in wardrobe has to be planned around air. Open shelving, glass-fronted cabinets and air-conditioned or dehumidified rooms keep clothes, shoes and bags in perfect condition. We plan the layout in 3D with you, then build every section in our workshop for a precise, fast installation.

Frequently asked questions

How big does a room need to be for a walk-in wardrobe?

A single-sided walk-in works from about 1.5 m wide; a two-sided layout from about 2.1 m; and a U-shape or island layout from about 3 m × 3 m. We can also convert part of a large bedroom into a walk-in closet with a partition.

Should a walk-in wardrobe in Bali have air-conditioning?

We strongly recommend either air-conditioning, a dedicated dehumidifier or excellent cross-ventilation. Without it, humidity can damage leather, shoes and delicate fabrics. We can integrate vents and dehumidifier housings into the design.

Can you add lighting and power?

Yes. We integrate LED strips, motion sensors, mirror lights and sockets for steamers or chargers, coordinating with your electrician.

Do you charge for a site visit and quote?

No. Site visits and quotes are free across our service areas in south Bali and Ubud. For remote locations we may agree a small travel fee in advance.

What deposit do you require?

Typically 50% to start production, 40% when pieces are ready for installation and 10% on completion. Exact terms are written in your quote.

Can you work from my architect's or designer's drawings?

Yes. We regularly build from architects' and interior designers' drawings and can prepare shop drawings for approval before production.
