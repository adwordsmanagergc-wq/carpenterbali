Wooden doors & windows in Sanur

Replacing doors or building new in Sanur?

WhatsApp usGet a free quote

Carpenter Bali makes solid timber doors, window frames, louvres and folding doors for villas and homes in Sanur, built from kiln-dried hardwood to stay straight in Bali's climate.

Doors and windows have to fight Bali's weather every day, driving rain, intense sun, humidity and termites. We make them in kiln-dried solid teak, merbau or bengkirai, with proper joinery, so they stay straight and keep closing smoothly.

We build entrance and pivot doors, interior doors, folding and sliding doors to gardens and pools, window frames (kusen), louvred shutters and traditional carved Balinese doors, supplied as complete sets with frames, hardware and finishing.

Materials & finishes

Kiln-dried solid teak (jati), merbau or bengkirai; engineered cores for large doors to prevent warping; stainless steel or brass hinges and locks; clear or tempered glass; marine-grade oil, polyurethane or exterior paint.

Local conditions: Sanur

Sanur's sheltered east-coast location is humid and salty near the beach. We use moisture-resistant boards, sealed edges and corrosion-resistant hardware.

Popular styles in Sanur

Sanur interiors often balance classic Balinese teak and carving with comfortable, practical modern kitchens and storage.

Homes & projects in Sanur

Sanur has many long-term family homes, older villas with classic Balinese architecture, and new residences, homes where owners value durability and comfort.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a teak door cost in Sanur?

A solid teak interior door with frame in Sanur starts from around Rp 6-12 million. Large entrance and pivot doors are quoted per design.

Can you supply doors and frames for a new villa build in Sanur?

Yes. We supply and install complete door and window packages for new builds in Sanur, working from your architect's schedule.

Why do wooden doors warp in Bali, and how do you prevent it?

Warping happens when wood isn't dried properly or is sealed on one side only. We use kiln-dried timber, stable engineered cores for large doors, and seal all six faces, including top and bottom edges, before installation.

Do you install doors and windows in existing villas?

Yes. We replace old doors and frames in existing buildings, including removal, adjusting openings and making good around the new frame.

Do you renovate older homes in Sanur?

Yes. We refresh kitchens, wardrobes and doors in older Sanur homes while keeping their character.

Can you design accessible kitchens and storage?

Yes. We design at comfortable heights with pull-out storage and easy-grip hardware, ideal for retirees.
