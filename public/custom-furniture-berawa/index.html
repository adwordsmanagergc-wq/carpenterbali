Custom furniture in Berawa

Furnishing a home, villa or restaurant in Berawa?

WhatsApp usGet a free quote

Carpenter Bali builds custom tables, daybeds, banquettes and outdoor furniture made to measure and delivered in Berawa.

Beyond built-ins, we make loose furniture to your design or ours: solid teak and suar dining tables, outdoor daybeds and loungers, sofa frames, coffee tables, benches, sideboards and consoles.

Send us a photo, a Pinterest board or a drawing, we'll suggest the right timber and finish for indoor or outdoor use and build it to your exact dimensions.

Materials & finishes

Plantation teak, suar, recycled teak, merbau, mango wood and rattan; steel and terrazzo combinations; oil, lacquer or outdoor marine finishes.

Local conditions: Berawa

Berawa sits close to the beach, so salt air and humidity are constant. We use marine-grade plywood, sealed edges and corrosion-resistant hardware throughout.

Popular styles in Berawa

Bright, family-friendly interiors with white or light oak cabinetry, rattan accents and durable HPL finishes are popular in Berawa, alongside more luxurious teak villas near the beach.

Homes & projects in Berawa

Berawa has a mix of family villas, long-stay rentals and newer gated communities. Families here often need extra storage, wardrobes, bunk beds, study desks and laundry cabinets.
