Pergolas, gazebos & bale in Nusa Dua

Want more shade and outdoor living space in Nusa Dua?

WhatsApp usGet a free quote

Carpenter Bali builds timber pergolas, poolside gazebos, carports and traditional Balinese bale for villas and resorts in Nusa Dua.

Shade is everything in Bali. We design and build timber pergolas over terraces and pools, carports, poolside gazebos and traditional Balinese bale, open-sided pavilions perfect for lounging, massage or outdoor dining.

Structures are built from durable hardwoods with engineered joints and footings, and can be roofed with alang-alang grass thatch, ulin shingles, clay tiles, glass, polycarbonate or left open with climbing plants.

Materials & finishes

Solid teak, merbau, bengkirai or coconut wood posts and beams; galvanised or stainless connectors; concrete footings; alang-alang, ulin shingle, tile, tempered glass or polycarbonate roofing.

Local conditions: Nusa Dua

Coastal and sunny, Nusa Dua properties need corrosion-resistant hardware and UV-stable finishes, especially for outdoor joinery.

Popular styles in Nusa Dua

Elegant, resort-style interiors with natural timber, stone and clean lines are typical in Nusa Dua homes and hotels.

Homes & projects in Nusa Dua

Nusa Dua combines resort properties with quiet residential neighbourhoods and family villas popular with long-term expats.

Frequently asked questions

What does a pergola cost in Nusa Dua?

Open timber pergolas in Nusa Dua start from around Rp 1.8-3.5 million per m². Gazebos start from about Rp 35 million and traditional bale from about Rp 60 million.

How long does it take to build a bale in Nusa Dua?

Allow 4-8 weeks including fabrication, footings and roofing.

How long does an alang-alang roof last?

A well-installed alang-alang thatch typically lasts 5-8 years depending on exposure and maintenance. Ulin shingles last much longer, often 20 years or more.

Do I need a permit for a pergola or bale?

Small garden structures often don't, but rules vary by regency and property type. We recommend checking with your notary or building consultant; we can provide drawings to support an application.

Do you work for hotels in Nusa Dua?

Yes. We supply room joinery, reception desks and outdoor furniture for hospitality projects in Nusa Dua.

Do you cover Tanjung Benoa and Sawangan?

Yes, we work across Nusa Dua, Tanjung Benoa, Bualu and Sawangan.
