How it works

  1. 1

    Consultation

    Share photos, plans or inspiration on WhatsApp. We discuss style, budget and timing.

  2. 2

    Site visit & measure

    We measure on site, check walls, floors and moisture, and advise on materials.

  3. 3

    Design & quote

    You receive drawings or a 3D render plus an itemised written quote.

  4. 4

    Workshop build

    Your pieces are built and finished in our workshop. We send progress photos.

  5. 5

    Installation

    Our own team installs, adjusts and cleans up. We walk you through care and maintenance.

  6. 6

    Aftercare

    Workmanship warranty and a check-up visit to adjust hinges and doors after the first wet season.

Frequently asked questions

Can you renovate an older villa in Legian?

Yes. We replace kitchens, wardrobes, vanities and doors to bring older Legian villas up to date.

Do you build space-saving storage for apartments in Legian?

Yes. We design built-in wardrobes, beds with storage and compact kitchens for apartments.

Do you charge for a site visit and quote?

No. Site visits and quotes are free across our service areas in south Bali and Ubud. For remote locations we may agree a small travel fee in advance.

What deposit do you require?

Typically 50% to start production, 40% when pieces are ready for installation and 10% on completion. Exact terms are written in your quote.

Can you work from my architect's or designer's drawings?

Yes. We regularly build from architects' and interior designers' drawings and can prepare shop drawings for approval before production.
