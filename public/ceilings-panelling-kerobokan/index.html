Ceilings & wall panelling in Kerobokan

Want warmth and texture in your space in Kerobokan? Carpenter Bali installs timber ceilings, slatted feature walls and acoustic panels in villas, restaurants and offices in Kerobokan.

Timber ceilings and wall panels add warmth, texture and acoustics to any space. We install tongue-and-groove and slatted ceilings, feature walls behind beds and TVs, acoustic slat panels for restaurants and offices, and exterior cladding.

Choose from solid teak, merbau, bamboo, pine or veneer panels, with integrated lighting, hidden access hatches and ventilation for the tropics.

Local conditions: Kerobokan

Inland Kerobokan is slightly less salty than the coast but still very humid, with occasional flooding in the wet season. Raised plinths and moisture-resistant boards are essential.

Popular styles in Kerobokan

Kerobokan homes often blend classic Balinese teak and carved details with modern kitchens and practical family storage.

Homes & projects in Kerobokan

Kerobokan offers larger plots, family houses, long-term rental villas and a growing number of renovated older homes — perfect for joinery upgrades that add real value.

What we build

Timber ceilings

Tongue-and-groove, slatted or coffered ceilings.

Feature walls

Slatted or panelled walls behind beds, TVs and reception desks.

Acoustic panels

Slat panels on acoustic felt for restaurants and studios.

Exterior cladding

Hardwood or composite facade cladding.

Bamboo ceilings

Natural woven or split bamboo for a tropical touch.

Integrated lighting

Recessed LEDs, cove lighting and access hatches.

Materials & finishes

Teak, merbau, pine, bamboo or veneered plywood; treated timber battens; acoustic felt backing; oil, lacquer or stain finishes.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a timber ceiling cost in Kerobokan?

Timber slat ceilings in Kerobokan start from around Rp 600,000–1.2 million per m², and bamboo ceilings from about Rp 350,000 per m².

How long does panelling installation take in Kerobokan?

Most rooms are completed in 2–5 days on site after fabrication.

Will a wooden ceiling attract termites?

We treat all timber and battens with anti-termite preservative and leave inspection access. Combined with a professional termite barrier for the building, timber ceilings are very durable.

Can wall panels improve acoustics?

Yes. Slatted panels on acoustic felt noticeably reduce echo in restaurants, offices and open-plan villas.

Do you upgrade long-term rental homes in Kerobokan?

Yes. We replace kitchens, wardrobes and doors in long-term rentals to raise their value and reduce maintenance.

Do you work in Kerobokan Kelod and Kaja?

Yes, all of Kerobokan, including Kerobokan Kelod, Kerobokan Kaja and Banjar Taman.
