TV & media units in Umalas

Want a clean, built-in TV wall in Umalas?

WhatsApp usGet a free quote

Carpenter Bali designs and builds custom media consoles and entertainment walls for living rooms, bedrooms and outdoor lounges in Umalas.

A custom TV wall pulls a living room together. We design floating media consoles, full-height entertainment walls with shelving and display niches, and slatted timber feature walls that hide cables, routers and speakers.

Every unit is planned around your screen size, devices, ventilation for electronics and the best viewing height, and finished to match your floors, doors and furniture.

Materials & finishes

Plywood carcasses with HPL, veneer or painted finishes; solid teak or merbau slats; ventilated back panels; push-to-open or soft-close hardware; LED profiles.

Local conditions: Umalas

Surrounded by rice fields and gardens, Umalas villas experience high humidity and plenty of insects. We use termite-treated materials and ventilated designs throughout.

Popular styles in Umalas

Umalas families love warm, natural interiors: teak and oak tones, generous kitchens with islands, walk-in wardrobes and indoor-outdoor living spaces.

Homes & projects in Umalas

Umalas is known for spacious family villas with big gardens, many owner-occupied for the long term, ideal for investing in high-quality, lasting joinery.

Frequently asked questions

What does a custom TV unit cost in Umalas?

Floating consoles in Umalas start from around Rp 5-10 million, and full-height TV walls from about Rp 12 million, depending on size and finish.

How long does a TV wall take in Umalas?

Typically 3-5 weeks to build, with installation in Umalas in one day.

Can you hide all the cables?

Yes. We plan cable channels, grommets and vented compartments for routers, consoles and sound systems, and coordinate with your electrician for sockets behind the screen.

Will the unit support a large TV?

We design the wall structure and fixings for your screen size and recommend a proper TV bracket fixed into masonry or a reinforced panel.

Do you build large family kitchens in Umalas?

Yes. Many Umalas projects include big kitchens with islands, pantries and outdoor BBQ kitchens.

Can you do a complete villa fit-out in Umalas?

Yes, we deliver complete joinery packages for new builds and renovations in Umalas.
