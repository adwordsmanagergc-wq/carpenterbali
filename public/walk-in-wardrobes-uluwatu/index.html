Walk-in wardrobes in Uluwatu

Dreaming of a walk-in wardrobe in Uluwatu? Carpenter Bali designs and builds luxury walk-in closets and dressing rooms for villas and family homes in Uluwatu, planned in 3D and built in our workshop to suit Bali's climate.

WhatsApp us Get a free quote

A walk-in wardrobe turns getting dressed into a daily pleasure. We design and build bespoke walk-in closets and dressing rooms for villas and family homes across Bali — from a compact walk-through between bedroom and ensuite to a boutique-style dressing room with a central island, display shelving and statement lighting.

In the tropics, a walk-in wardrobe has to be planned around air. Open shelving, glass-fronted cabinets and air-conditioned or dehumidified rooms keep clothes, shoes and bags in perfect condition. We plan the layout in 3D with you, then build every section in our workshop for a precise, fast installation.

Local conditions: Uluwatu

Uluwatu is drier than the rest of south Bali but exposed to strong sun, wind and salt spray on the cliffs. Outdoor joinery needs UV-resistant finishes, stainless fixings and timber chosen for weather resistance.

Popular styles in Uluwatu

Uluwatu style is coastal and raw: natural timber, limestone, concrete, rattan and bold indoor-outdoor living with ocean views.

Homes & projects in Uluwatu

The Bukit has seen a boom in modern villas, surf retreats and boutique hotels, many built with open-plan living, big glass doors and extensive outdoor decking.

Frequently asked questions

What does a walk-in wardrobe cost in Uluwatu?

Compact walk-through wardrobes in Uluwatu start from around Rp 25–45 million, while larger dressing rooms with islands and lighting typically start from Rp 90 million. Your quote depends on size, finishes and hardware.

How long does a walk-in closet project take in Uluwatu?

Allow 5–9 weeks from approved 3D design to completion. On-site installation in Uluwatu usually takes 3–5 days.

Can you design a walk-in wardrobe for a villa still under construction in Uluwatu?

Yes — and it's the best time. We can coordinate with your builder on wall positions, electrical points and air-conditioning so the walk-in closet is perfectly integrated.

How big does a room need to be for a walk-in wardrobe?

A single-sided walk-in works from about 1.5 m wide; a two-sided layout from about 2.1 m; and a U-shape or island layout from about 3 m × 3 m. We can also convert part of a large bedroom into a walk-in closet with a partition.

Should a walk-in wardrobe in Bali have air-conditioning?

We strongly recommend either air-conditioning, a dedicated dehumidifier or excellent cross-ventilation. Without it, humidity can damage leather, shoes and delicate fabrics. We can integrate vents and dehumidifier housings into the design.

Do you work in Bingin, Padang Padang and Pecatu?

Yes, we cover the whole Uluwatu area, including Bingin, Padang Padang, Pecatu, Suluban, Labuan Sait and Dreamland.

What materials do you recommend for cliffside villas?

For outdoor areas we recommend ulin, merbau or teak with UV oil, stainless steel fixings and composite decking in very exposed spots.
