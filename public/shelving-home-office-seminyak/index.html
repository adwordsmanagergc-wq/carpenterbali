Home offices & shelving in Seminyak

Working remotely in Seminyak? Carpenter Bali builds built-in desks, home offices, bookcases and storage for villas and apartments in Seminyak.

Bali is home to thousands of remote workers, and a proper workspace changes everything. We build ergonomic home offices with built-in desks, cable management, storage and shelving — tucked into a bedroom corner or filling a dedicated study.

We also design bookcases, alcove shelving, under-stair storage, laundry cabinets and utility storage that make the most of every metre.

Local conditions: Seminyak

Seminyak's beachfront location means salt-laden air and high humidity. Hardware corrosion and swelling of cheap boards are common problems we fix — and prevent with the right materials.

Popular styles in Seminyak

Seminyak favours refined luxury: dark teak with brass, terrazzo and marble, boutique-hotel style walk-in closets, statement doors and polished commercial interiors.

Homes & projects in Seminyak

Seminyak properties range from established luxury villas due for renovation to compact private-pool villas run as holiday rentals, plus a dense mix of restaurants, bars and boutiques.

How it works

  1. 1

    Consultation

    Share photos, plans or inspiration on WhatsApp. We discuss style, budget and timing.

  2. 2

    Site visit & measure

    We measure on site, check walls, floors and moisture, and advise on materials.

  3. 3

    Design & quote

    You receive drawings or a 3D render plus an itemised written quote.

  4. 4

    Workshop build

    Your pieces are built and finished in our workshop. We send progress photos.

  5. 5

    Installation

    Our own team installs, adjusts and cleans up. We walk you through care and maintenance.

  6. 6

    Aftercare

    Workmanship warranty and a check-up visit to adjust hinges and doors after the first wet season.
