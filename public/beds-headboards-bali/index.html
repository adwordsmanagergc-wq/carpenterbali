Custom beds & bedroom furniture in Bali

Custom beds, headboards, bedside tables and bunk beds for villas and family homes.

WhatsApp usGet a free quote

We build beds and bedroom furniture that make villas feel like boutique hotels: solid teak platform beds, wall-to-wall headboards with integrated bedside shelves and reading lights, rattan and upholstered headboards, and space-saving bunk beds for kids' rooms.

Beds are made to standard mattress sizes or fully custom dimensions, with storage drawers, lift-up bases and slatted platforms that let mattresses breathe in the humidity.

Materials & finishes

Solid plantation teak, merbau or plywood with veneer/HPL; slatted bases for airflow; natural rattan or performance fabrics; integrated LED reading lights.

How it works

  1. Consultation

    Share photos, plans or inspiration on WhatsApp. We discuss style, budget and timing.

  2. Site visit & measure

    We measure on site, check walls, floors and moisture, and advise on materials.

  3. Design & quote

    You receive drawings or a 3D render plus an itemised written quote.

  4. Workshop build

    Your pieces are built and finished in our workshop. We send progress photos.

  5. Installation

    Our own team installs, adjusts and cleans up. We walk you through care and maintenance.

  6. Aftercare

    Workmanship warranty and a check-up visit to adjust hinges and doors after the first wet season.

Frequently asked questions

Can you make beds for European or US mattress sizes?

Yes. We build to any mattress size, Indonesian, European, UK, US or Australian, or fully custom dimensions.

Why do you recommend slatted bases?

Slatted bases let air circulate under the mattress, which prevents mould and mildew in Bali's humidity.

Do you charge for a site visit and quote?

No. Site visits and quotes are free across our service areas in south Bali and Ubud. For remote locations we may agree a small travel fee in advance.

What deposit do you require?

Typically 50% to start production, 40% when pieces are ready for installation and 10% on completion. Exact terms are written in your quote.

Can you work from my architect's or designer's drawings?

Yes. We regularly build from architects' and interior designers' drawings and can prepare shop drawings for approval before production.
