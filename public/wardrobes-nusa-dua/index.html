Built-in wardrobes in Nusa Dua

Need wardrobes in Nusa Dua? Carpenter Bali builds custom, floor-to-ceiling built-in wardrobes for villas, apartments and rental properties in Nusa Dua, designed to keep clothes dry and fresh in Bali's humid climate.

A good wardrobe in Bali does more than store clothes — it protects them. In a closed cupboard, tropical humidity quickly turns into mould and musty smells. Our built-in wardrobes are designed to breathe: ventilated plinths and tops, rattan or louvred door options, moisture-resistant boards inside and space for a dehumidifier or a low-wattage warm light.

We build floor-to-ceiling fitted wardrobes for master bedrooms, guest rooms, kids' rooms and rental villas, with hanging space, drawers, shoe racks, safes and luggage storage planned around how you live. Choose from sliding doors, hinged doors, Balinese rattan panels, teak louvres, mirrored fronts or seamless painted finishes.

Local conditions: Nusa Dua

Coastal and sunny, Nusa Dua properties need corrosion-resistant hardware and UV-stable finishes, especially for outdoor joinery.

Popular styles in Nusa Dua

Elegant, resort-style interiors with natural timber, stone and clean lines are typical in Nusa Dua homes and hotels.

Homes & projects in Nusa Dua

Nusa Dua combines resort properties with quiet residential neighbourhoods and family villas popular with long-term expats.

What we build

Sliding & hinged doors

Smooth top-hung or bottom-rolling sliders, or hinged doors with soft-close.

Rattan & louvred fronts

Breathable natural fronts that look beautiful and prevent mould.

Ventilation built in

Smart interiors

Mirrored & glass doors

Rental-ready designs

Materials & finishes

Carcasses in 18 mm plywood (marine-grade in bathrooms and coastal villas), with HPL or melamine-faced interiors for easy cleaning. Doors in HPL, two-pack paint, teak veneer, solid teak frames with rattan, or mirror. Sliding systems and soft-close hinges rated for tropical use.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a built-in wardrobe cost in Nusa Dua?

A full-height plywood and HPL wardrobe in Nusa Dua starts from around Rp 3–5 million per linear metre. Teak, rattan and painted finishes cost more. We give a free itemised quote after measuring your room.

How quickly can you install wardrobes in Nusa Dua?

Most wardrobe projects take 3–6 weeks from design approval, and installation in Nusa Dua usually takes one or two days per room.

Can you match wardrobes to my existing villa style in Nusa Dua?

Yes. We match timber tones, door styles and hardware to your existing interiors, whether that is classic Balinese teak, tropical-modern or minimalist white.

How do you stop mould inside wardrobes in Bali?

We design wardrobes to ventilate: vented plinths and tops, small gaps behind back panels so air can circulate off the wall, breathable rattan or louvred doors where suitable, and wipe-clean interiors. A small dehumidifier or a low-heat LED strip inside makes a big difference too.

Sliding or hinged doors — which is better?

Sliding doors save space in smaller bedrooms and suit modern designs. Hinged doors give full access to the whole wardrobe at once and are easier to fit with rattan or louvred panels. We'll recommend what suits your room after measuring.

Do you work for hotels in Nusa Dua?

Yes. We supply room joinery, reception desks and outdoor furniture for hospitality projects in Nusa Dua.

Do you cover Tanjung Benoa and Sawangan?

Yes, we work across Nusa Dua, Tanjung Benoa, Bualu and Sawangan.
