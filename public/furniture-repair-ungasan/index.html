Furniture repair & restoration in Ungasan

Need a carpenter for repairs in Ungasan? Carpenter Bali fixes swollen cabinets, sticking doors, broken hinges and termite damage, and restores teak furniture for homes and villas in Ungasan.

Bali's climate is hard on wood, and even good furniture needs care. We repair swollen kitchen cabinets, sticking doors and drawers, broken hinges and runners, termite-damaged timber, and water-damaged vanities.

We also restore and refinish teak furniture, re-oil decks and doors, and refresh rental villas between seasons — often far cheaper than replacing.

Local conditions: Ungasan

Ungasan is dry, sunny and windy with salty sea air. We focus on UV-stable outdoor finishes, sealed indoor joinery and stainless or brass hardware.

Popular styles in Ungasan

High-end tropical luxury: teak and stone, walk-in dressing rooms, statement entrance doors and expansive outdoor decks.

Homes & projects in Ungasan

Ungasan villas tend to be large, high-end properties — many with multiple bedrooms, infinity pools and expansive terraces — where quality joinery is expected.

Frequently asked questions

How quickly can a carpenter come for repairs in Ungasan?

We can usually schedule an assessment visit in Ungasan within a few working days, and urgent jobs sooner when the team is nearby.

How much do carpentry repairs cost in Ungasan?

An assessment visit in Ungasan starts from around Rp 300,000, which is credited to the job if you go ahead. Repairs are then quoted per item.

Can swollen kitchen cabinets be repaired?

Often yes. If the carcass is sound we replace the swollen doors, panels or plinths in moisture-resistant materials. If the carcass is particle board that has failed, replacement in plywood is usually the better long-term option.

Do you offer maintenance contracts for rental villas?

Yes. We offer scheduled visits to adjust doors, replace worn hardware, re-oil timber and fix guest damage.

Do you build luxury walk-in wardrobes in Ungasan?

Yes. Many of our Ungasan projects include walk-in closets and dressing rooms designed in 3D.

Do you cover Kutuh and Melasti?

Yes, we work throughout Ungasan, Kutuh, Melasti and Sawangan.
