Walk-in wardrobes in Denpasar

Dreaming of a walk-in wardrobe in Denpasar?

WhatsApp usGet a free quote

Carpenter Bali designs and builds luxury walk-in closets and dressing rooms for villas and family homes in Denpasar, planned in 3D and built in our workshop to suit Bali's climate.

A walk-in wardrobe turns getting dressed into a daily pleasure. We design and build bespoke walk-in closets and dressing rooms for villas and family homes across Bali, from a compact walk-through between bedroom and ensuite to a boutique-style dressing room with a central island, display shelving and statement lighting.

In the tropics, a walk-in wardrobe has to be planned around air. Open shelving, glass-fronted cabinets and air-conditioned or dehumidified rooms keep clothes, shoes and bags in perfect condition. We plan the layout in 3D with you, then build every section in our workshop for a precise, fast installation.

Materials & finishes

Plywood carcasses with premium veneer, HPL or two-pack painted finishes; solid teak or oak-look accents; aluminium-framed glass doors; leather or fabric-lined drawers; LED profiles with sensors; soft-close, full-extension runners.

Local conditions: Denpasar

Urban Denpasar is hot and humid. Moisture-resistant boards and good ventilation keep cabinets and wardrobes in shape.

Popular styles in Denpasar

Clean modern designs, practical storage and professional office and retail interiors are most requested in Denpasar.

Homes & projects in Denpasar

Denpasar has a mix of family houses, shophouses, offices and clinics, projects where practical, durable joinery and good value matter.

Frequently asked questions

What does a walk-in wardrobe cost in Denpasar?

Compact walk-through wardrobes in Denpasar start from around Rp 25-45 million, while larger dressing rooms with islands and lighting typically start from Rp 90 million. Your quote depends on size, finishes and hardware.

How long does a walk-in closet project take in Denpasar?

Allow 5-9 weeks from approved 3D design to completion. On-site installation in Denpasar usually takes 3-5 days.

Can you design a walk-in wardrobe for a villa still under construction in Denpasar?

Yes, and it's the best time. We can coordinate with your builder on wall positions, electrical points and air-conditioning so the walk-in closet is perfectly integrated.

How big does a room need to be for a walk-in wardrobe?

A single-sided walk-in works from about 1.5 m wide; a two-sided layout from about 2.1 m; and a U-shape or island layout from about 3 m × 3 m. We can also convert part of a large bedroom into a walk-in closet with a partition.

Should a walk-in wardrobe in Bali have air-conditioning?

We strongly recommend either air-conditioning, a dedicated dehumidifier or excellent cross-ventilation. Without it, humidity can damage leather, shoes and delicate fabrics. We can integrate vents and dehumidifier housings into the design.

Do you do office and shop fit-outs in Denpasar?

Yes. We build reception desks, office desks, storage and retail displays for businesses in Denpasar.

Do you cover Renon and Sesetan?

Yes, we work throughout Denpasar, including Renon, Sesetan, Panjer, Sidakarya and Pemogan.
