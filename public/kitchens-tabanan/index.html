Custom kitchens in Tabanan

Looking for a custom kitchen in Tabanan? Carpenter Bali designs, builds and installs made-to-measure kitchen sets for villas, homes, rentals and restaurants in Tabanan — from compact kitchenettes to full kitchens with islands and walk-in pantries, all built to handle Bali's humidity.

The kitchen is the heart of every villa — and the room that suffers most in Bali's climate. Steam, splashes and tropical humidity destroy chipboard cabinets in months. We build kitchens on moisture-resistant marine plywood carcasses with sealed edges, stainless or aluminium plinths and quality soft-close hardware, so doors stay aligned and drawers keep gliding year after year.

Whether you want a minimalist handle-less kitchen, a warm solid-teak tropical kitchen, a Japandi look in oak-tone veneer or a high-gloss painted finish, we design around your layout, appliances and the way you cook. We also build butler's pantries, kitchen islands with seating, open-air outdoor kitchens and compact kitchenettes for rental villas.

Local conditions: Tabanan

Rice-field surroundings and coastal breezes make Tabanan very humid. We specify termite-treated timber, marine plywood and ventilated designs as standard.

Popular styles in Tabanan

Earthy, natural interiors with timber, rattan, stone and big openings to the rice fields are typical of new Tabanan villas.

Homes & projects in Tabanan

Tabanan is dominated by new-build villas, eco-retreats and developments surrounded by rice fields — perfect for joinery planned during construction.

Materials & finishes

Carcasses in 18 mm marine-grade (WBP) plywood, sealed on all edges. Fronts in HPL, two-pack (duco) paint, teak veneer, or solid teak. Hardware from Blum, Hettich or Hafele-grade equivalents with soft-close. Plinths in aluminium or stainless steel to resist mopping water.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a custom kitchen cost in Tabanan?

A typical plywood and HPL kitchen in Tabanan starts from around Rp 3.5–5 million per linear metre for base and wall units. Painted finishes and solid teak cost more. After a free site visit we give you an itemised quote, so you know exactly what you are paying for.

How long does it take to get a new kitchen in Tabanan?

Most kitchens take 4–8 weeks from approved design to installation, depending on size and finish. Installation on site in Tabanan usually takes 2–4 days, plus countertop fitting.

Can you replace an old kitchen in a rental villa in Tabanan quickly?

Yes. For rental villas we can plan the build so the old kitchen is removed and the new one installed within a short window, minimising lost booking nights.

What is the best material for a kitchen in Bali?

For the cabinet boxes, marine-grade plywood is the most reliable choice in Bali's humidity — far more stable than MDF or particle board. For doors, HPL laminate is the most durable and easiest to clean, two-pack paint gives a seamless modern look, and solid teak gives the warmest tropical character.

Do you install countertops and appliances?

We supply and fit countertops in terrazzo, granite, quartz, marble or solid surface through trusted stone partners, and we can install built-in appliances, sinks and taps in coordination with your plumber and electrician.

Do you work in Kedungu and Nyanyi?

Yes, we regularly work in Kedungu, Nyanyi, Tanah Lot, Kaba-Kaba, Cepaka and Buwit.

Can you supply joinery for an off-plan villa in Tabanan?

Yes. We work with builders to plan joinery during construction for a clean, integrated finish.
