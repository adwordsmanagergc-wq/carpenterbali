Timber staircases in Kerobokan

Planning a staircase in Kerobokan? Carpenter Bali builds floating stairs, timber stair cladding and railings for villas and homes in Kerobokan.

WhatsApp us Get a free quote

A staircase is often the first thing you see in a villa. We build floating stairs with solid teak or merbau treads, clad concrete stairs in timber, and make complete timber staircases for lofts and mezzanines.

We combine timber with steel stringers, glass balustrades, rope or slatted railings to suit tropical-modern, industrial or classic Balinese interiors — always designed for safety and stability.

Local conditions: Kerobokan

Inland Kerobokan is slightly less salty than the coast but still very humid, with occasional flooding in the wet season. Raised plinths and moisture-resistant boards are essential.

Popular styles in Kerobokan

Kerobokan homes often blend classic Balinese teak and carved details with modern kitchens and practical family storage.

Homes & projects in Kerobokan

Kerobokan offers larger plots, family houses, long-term rental villas and a growing number of renovated older homes — perfect for joinery upgrades that add real value.

What we build

Floating stairs

Thick solid treads cantilevered from the wall or on a hidden steel spine.

Stair cladding

Timber treads and risers fitted over concrete stairs.

Loft & mezzanine stairs

Space-saving and alternating-tread designs.

Railings & balustrades

Timber, steel, glass, rope or slatted screens.

Spiral stairs

Compact spiral and helical stairs for tight spaces.

Anti-slip finishes

Grooved nosings and anti-slip oils for safety.

Materials & finishes

Solid teak, merbau or bengkirai treads (40–80 mm); steel stringers or spines; tempered glass, steel or timber balustrades; hard-wearing polyurethane or oil finishes.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a floating staircase cost in Kerobokan?

Floating stairs in Kerobokan typically start from around Rp 2.5–5 million per step, including treads and fixings. Cladding existing stairs starts from about Rp 1.2 million per step.

How long does stair installation take in Kerobokan?

Usually 2–4 days on site after 4–7 weeks of fabrication.

Can you clad my existing concrete stairs in wood?

Yes. We measure each step individually and make solid timber treads and risers that are fixed and sealed onto the concrete.

Are floating stairs safe?

Yes, when engineered and fixed correctly. We use thick solid treads and fix them into structural walls or a steel spine, and recommend a balustrade wherever there's a drop.

Do you upgrade long-term rental homes in Kerobokan?

Yes. We replace kitchens, wardrobes and doors in long-term rentals to raise their value and reduce maintenance.

Do you work in Kerobokan Kelod and Kaja?

Yes, all of Kerobokan, including Kerobokan Kelod, Kerobokan Kaja and Banjar Taman.
