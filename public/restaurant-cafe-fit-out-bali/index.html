Restaurant, café & retail fit-out in Bali

Bars, counters, banquettes, shelving and joinery for restaurants, cafés, shops and hotels.

WhatsApp usGet a free quote

Hospitality is Bali's biggest industry, and the interior is a big part of the brand. We build bar counters, coffee counters, banquette seating, back-bar shelving, host stands, retail displays, hotel room joinery and reception desks.

Commercial joinery has to take heavy daily use. We specify hard-wearing surfaces, commercial-grade hardware and finishes that clean easily, and we work to tight opening dates.

Materials & finishes

Commercial-grade plywood, compact laminate, HPL, solid hardwood, stainless steel, stone and terrazzo; heavy-duty hardware; food-safe and easy-clean finishes.

What we build

Bar & coffee counters
Durable counters with stone, terrazzo or timber tops.
Banquettes & booths
Built-in seating frames ready for upholstery.
Back-bar shelving
Display shelving with integrated lighting.
Retail displays
Shelving, display tables and cash counters for boutiques.
Hotel joinery
Room packages: wardrobes, desks, headboards and minibars.

Statement reception desks for hotels, gyms and offices.

How it works

  1. Consultation

    Share photos, plans or inspiration on WhatsApp. We discuss style, budget and timing.

  2. Site visit & measure

    We measure on site, check walls, floors and moisture, and advise on materials.

  3. Design & quote

    You receive drawings or a 3D render plus an itemised written quote.

  4. Workshop build

    Your pieces are built and finished in our workshop. We send progress photos.

  5. Installation

    Our own team installs, adjusts and cleans up. We walk you through care and maintenance.

  6. Aftercare

    Workmanship warranty and a check-up visit to adjust hinges and doors after the first wet season.

Frequently asked questions

Can you meet a fixed opening date?

Yes. We agree a programme up front, build off-site in our workshop and install in the shortest possible window.

Do you build to a designer's concept?

Yes. We regularly work from interior designers' concepts and develop the shop drawings and material samples for approval.

Do you charge for a site visit and quote?

No. Site visits and quotes are free across our service areas in south Bali and Ubud. For remote locations we may agree a small travel fee in advance.

What deposit do you require?

Typically 50% to start production, 40% when pieces are ready for installation and 10% on completion. Exact terms are written in your quote.

Can you work from my architect's or designer's drawings?

Yes. We regularly build from architects' and interior designers' drawings and can prepare shop drawings for approval before production.
