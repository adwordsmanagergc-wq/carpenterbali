Built-in wardrobes in Legian

Need wardrobes in Legian? Carpenter Bali builds custom, floor-to-ceiling built-in wardrobes for villas, apartments and rental properties in Legian, designed to keep clothes dry and fresh in Bali's humid climate.

WhatsApp us Get a free quote

A good wardrobe in Bali does more than store clothes — it protects them. In a closed cupboard, tropical humidity quickly turns into mould and musty smells. Our built-in wardrobes are designed to breathe: ventilated plinths and tops, rattan or louvred door options, moisture-resistant boards inside and space for a dehumidifier or a low-wattage warm light.

We build floor-to-ceiling fitted wardrobes for master bedrooms, guest rooms, kids' rooms and rental villas, with hanging space, drawers, shoe racks, safes and luggage storage planned around how you live. Choose from sliding doors, hinged doors, Balinese rattan panels, teak louvres, mirrored fronts or seamless painted finishes.

Local conditions: Legian

Close to the beach, Legian properties face salt air and humidity. We replace failing chipboard joinery with marine-grade materials built to last.

Popular styles in Legian

Clean modern designs with timber accents are most popular in Legian, as owners refresh older properties for today's guests.

Homes & projects in Legian

Legian has many older villas and hotels ready for renovation, as well as compact apartments where clever built-in storage makes a big difference.

Materials & finishes

Carcasses in 18 mm plywood (marine-grade in bathrooms and coastal villas), with HPL or melamine-faced interiors for easy cleaning. Doors in HPL, two-pack paint, teak veneer, solid teak frames with rattan, or mirror. Sliding systems and soft-close hinges rated for tropical use.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a built-in wardrobe cost in Legian?

A full-height plywood and HPL wardrobe in Legian starts from around Rp 3–5 million per linear metre. Teak, rattan and painted finishes cost more. We give a free itemised quote after measuring your room.

How quickly can you install wardrobes in Legian?

Most wardrobe projects take 3–6 weeks from design approval, and installation in Legian usually takes one or two days per room.

Can you match wardrobes to my existing villa style in Legian?

Yes. We match timber tones, door styles and hardware to your existing interiors, whether that is classic Balinese teak, tropical-modern or minimalist white.

How do you stop mould inside wardrobes in Bali?

We design wardrobes to ventilate: vented plinths and tops, small gaps behind back panels so air can circulate off the wall, breathable rattan or louvred doors where suitable, and wipe-clean interiors. A small dehumidifier or a low-heat LED strip inside makes a big difference too.

Sliding or hinged doors — which is better?

Sliding doors save space in smaller bedrooms and suit modern designs. Hinged doors give full access to the whole wardrobe at once and are easier to fit with rattan or louvred panels. We'll recommend what suits your room after measuring.

Can you renovate an older villa in Legian?

Yes. We replace kitchens, wardrobes, vanities and doors to bring older Legian villas up to date.

Do you build space-saving storage for apartments in Legian?

Yes. We design built-in wardrobes, beds with storage and compact kitchens for apartments.
