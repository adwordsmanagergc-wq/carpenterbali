Wooden doors & windows in Pererenan

Replacing doors or building new in Pererenan? Carpenter Bali makes solid timber doors, window frames, louvres and folding doors for villas and homes in Pererenan, built from kiln-dried hardwood to stay straight in Bali's climate.

Doors and windows have to fight Bali's weather every day — driving rain, intense sun, humidity and termites. We make them in kiln-dried solid teak, merbau or bengkirai, with proper joinery, so they stay straight and keep closing smoothly.

We build entrance and pivot doors, interior doors, folding and sliding doors to gardens and pools, window frames (kusen), louvred shutters and traditional carved Balinese doors — supplied as complete sets with frames, hardware and finishing.

Local conditions: Pererenan

With the beach on one side and rice fields on the other, Pererenan villas face salt air, high humidity and heavy wet-season rain. We specify sealed marine-grade materials and termite-treated timber as standard.

Popular styles in Pererenan

Pererenan clients often choose warm minimalism: natural timber veneers, limewash and microcement, soft curves, rattan details and integrated lighting.

Homes & projects in Pererenan

Pererenan has many brand-new villas and off-plan developments, which makes it ideal for coordinated joinery packages planned during construction rather than after.

What we build

Entrance & pivot doors

Solid, panelled, flush or slatted doors with matching frames.

Materials & finishes

Kiln-dried solid teak (jati), merbau or bengkirai; engineered cores for large doors to prevent warping; stainless steel or brass hinges and locks; clear or tempered glass; marine-grade oil, polyurethane or exterior paint.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a teak door cost in Pererenan?

A solid teak interior door with frame in Pererenan starts from around Rp 6–12 million. Large entrance and pivot doors are quoted per design.

Can you supply doors and frames for a new villa build in Pererenan?

Yes. We supply and install complete door and window packages for new builds in Pererenan, working from your architect's schedule.

Why do wooden doors warp in Bali, and how do you prevent it?

Warping happens when wood isn't dried properly or is sealed on one side only. We use kiln-dried timber, stable engineered cores for large doors, and seal all six faces — including top and bottom edges — before installation.

Do you install doors and windows in existing villas?

Yes. We replace old doors and frames in existing buildings, including removal, adjusting openings and making good around the new frame.

Do you work on new villa developments in Pererenan?

Yes. We work with developers and builders in Pererenan to supply complete joinery packages to programme.

Do you also cover Seseh and Cemagi?

Yes, we regularly work in Seseh, Cemagi and Munggu, just beyond Pererenan.
